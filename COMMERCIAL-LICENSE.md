## 💡 Pricing Notes & FAQ

**Q1. Is a "per-instance" license one IP, one host, or one cluster node?**
A "production deployment / server instance" means one physical server, VM, or container running a single `biubo-waf` process. For cluster mode (multiple `biubo-waf` nodes sharing configuration / threat intel), please contact us for cluster licensing.

**Q2. Can I get a free trial?**
Yes — a **30-day free trial** of the Commercial License is available on request. Just email us with your use case. (The AGPL-3.0 release is also free to use, of course, with its own source-disclosure obligations.)

**Q3. Do I have to pay for development / staging environments?**
A single non-production environment (staging, dev, QA) per commercial license is included at no extra cost. Production environments each require their own instance.

**Q4. What happens if I stop paying the subscription?**
You lose the right to use **new versions** released after your subscription ends. You may continue to use the **last version released during your active subscription** indefinitely, under the terms of your original subscription agreement.

**Q5. Do you offer education / non-profit / open-source project discounts?**
Yes. Educational institutions, registered non-profits, and other open-source projects can request a **50% discount** on subscription and buyout plans. Please include verification documents in your inquiry.

**Q6. Can I pay in CNY or USD?**
Yes — we accept **CNY (¥)** for Chinese customers (via bank transfer / WeChat Pay / Alipay) and **USD ($)** for international customers (via bank wire / PayPal). Cryptocurrency is also available on request.

**Q7. Is this a quote, or a binding price list?**
The prices above are **indicative starting prices** and may vary based on your deployment size, contract length, and feature requirements. The final price will be confirmed in a written quotation and commercial license agreement.
